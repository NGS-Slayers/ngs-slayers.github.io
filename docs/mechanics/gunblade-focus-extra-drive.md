# <img src="/img/38px-NGSUISkillGunbladeFocusExtraDrive.png" alt="icon" className="heading-icon"/>Gunblade Focus Extra Drive
Introduced in the 04/01/2026 patch, [Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) allows you to consume 3 levels of [Gunblade Focus](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus) to gain one charge of Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot). This is a powerful burst damage tool that generates significant [Photon Blast](/moveset/photon-blast) gauge and instantly fills the [Unleashed Rage](/moveset/active-skills#unleashed-rage) meter.

While active, the skill also enhances your [PAs](/moveset/photon-arts) by adding a secondary though weaker [Relentless Blade](/moveset/relentless-blade) hit. Consequently, unless you are performing [Counters](/moveset/counters) or using [Unleashed Rage](/moveset/active-skills#unleashed-rage), your rotation should primarily consist of [Stay Arts Shifting Spica](/moveset/photon-arts#sss), [Stay Arts Waving Rigel](/moveset/photon-arts#swr), and [Stay Arts Flowing Sirius Stages 1 and 2](/moveset/photon-arts#sfs12).

[Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) has a duration of 30 seconds, during which you cannot enter [Gunblade Focus Overdrive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-overdrive). It has a 90-second cooldown, so plan your burst windows accordingly.

*   Wait until you reach [Gunblade Focus](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus) Level 5 before using the Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot), as using it earlier will result in lower damage output.
*   Avoid using the regular [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot) while [Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) is active. Only the Super version provides enough value to justify its use during this window.

Canceling [Stay Arts Flowing Sirius Stages 1 and 2](/moveset/photon-arts#sfs12) into [Weapon Action Stages 1-3](/moveset/weapon-action#wa123) remains a DPS increase, though canceling strictly into [Weapon Action Stage 1](/moveset/weapon-action#wa123) is even more effective.
