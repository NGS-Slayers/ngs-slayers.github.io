# <img src="/img/38px-NGSUISkillGunbladeFocusExtraDrive.png" alt="icon" className="heading-icon"/>Gunblade Focus Extra Drive
With the Patch from 04/01/2026 [Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) was introduced. For sacrificing 3 Levels of [Gunblade Focus](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus) it grants us one charge of a Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot) which is a great burst damage tool with great [Photon Blast](/moveset/photon-blast) generation and immediately fills the meter for [Unleashed Rage](/moveset/active-skills#unleashed-rage).

Additionally it enhances our [PAs](/moveset/photon-arts) by adding an additional but weaker [Relentless Blade](/moveset/relentless-blade) hit while the skill is active. This essentially means that outside of [Counters](/moveset/counters) and [Unleashed Rage](/moveset/active-skills#unleashed-rage) you should mostly use [Stay Arts Shifting Spica](/moveset/photon-arts#sss), [Stay Arts Waving Rigel](/moveset/photon-arts#swr) and [Stay Arts Flowing Sirius Stage 1 and 2](/moveset/photon-arts#sfs12).

[Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) lasts for 30 seconds in which you are unable to enter [Gunblade Focus Overdrive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-overdrive). It also has a 90 seconds cooldown so make sure to plan your burst windows accordingly.

* Don't use Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot) before reaching Gunblade Focus Level 5 again since it would lower the damage of your Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot).

* Avoid using regular [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot) especially while [Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive) is active. Only the Super [Slug Shot](/moveset/slug-shot) is worth using within duration of [Gunblade Focus Extra Drive](/skill-tree/skills#gunblade-focus-extra-drive).

Canceling [Stay Arts Flowing Sirius Stage 1 and 2](/moveset/photon-arts#sfs12) into [Weapon Action Stage 1 to 3](/moveset/weapon-action#wa123) is still an increase in DPS, canceling into only [Weapon Action Stage 1](/moveset/weapon-action#wa123) is even better.
